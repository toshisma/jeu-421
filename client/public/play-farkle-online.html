Play Farkle online, free, with friends

The Courtier is our version of Farkle, the classic six-dice push-your-luck game (also known as Ten Thousand or Zilch). Open a table, send your friends a code, and play together in the browser on your phone or computer. It’s free, there’s no download, and you don’t need an account to play.

How to play with friends

  1. Open The Courtier and choose Open a table.
  2. Share the 5-character code or the link. Your friends join from any browser, no install needed.
  3. Start the game when everyone is ready: 2 to 6 players at the table, and the host can add computer players to fill empty seats.

Every roll is drawn by the server, and all players see the same dice tumble in the 3D tray.

Ways to play

With friends online

A private table with a code or a link, for 2 to 6 players. Chat and quick reactions are built in.

Against the computer

1 to 3 opponents at three levels: Beginner, who pushes their luck too far; Regular, who plays sensibly; and Sharp, who calculates the odds.

Quick game

One tap to join a public table that’s waiting for players.

Your target

Play to 2,000, 4,000, 10,000 points. A two-player game takes about 3 turns each to 2,000, 6 to 7 turns to 4,000, and 17 to 18 turns to 10,000.

Durations come from thousands of simulated games between two Regular computers, run with the game’s own engine.

The rules we use

Roll six dice, set aside at least one scoring die, then roll again or bank your points. A roll with no scoring dice is a farkle: you lose the points from that turn. Set aside all six dice and you have hot dice: roll all six again. The first player to reach the target when banking wins immediately, with no final round. All the scoring is on the Farkle rules page.

Free or for Groschen

You can play every game for free. The Courtier also has its own play money, Groschen: with a free account you get 20 Groschen a day and can stake them on a game, winner takes the pot. Groschen are game currency only: no real money, they can’t be bought or cashed out.

A free account (a username and a password, no email address) also lets you add friends and keep your progress across devices.

English and French

Only The Courtier (Farkle) is available in English. The rest of 421online.com, including the 421 and Coups Fourrés games, is in French.

Inside the game, a small FR / EN switch changes the language of The Courtier at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Farkle game free?

Yes. There’s no subscription, no ads and nothing to buy. It runs in your browser on a phone or a computer.

Do my friends need an account to join?

No. They open your link or enter the table code, choose a name and play. An account is only needed for stakes in Groschen, friends lists and saved progress.

How many players can play Farkle online here?

From 2 to 6 players at an online table, or 1 to 3 computer opponents when you play alone.

Is the whole site in English?

No. Only The Courtier, the Farkle mode, is in English. The rest of 421online.com, including its other games, is in French.

Does it use real money?

No. Groschen are play money only: they can’t be bought or cashed out, and you can always play for free.
