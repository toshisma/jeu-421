Invite friends, step by step

  1. Open the game and pick 421, The Courtier (Farkle) or Poker menteur (liar’s dice).
  2. Create a private table: it gets a 5-letter code.
  3. Send the code (or the link) to your group chat.
  4. Start the game when everyone’s seated. Someone missing? The host can add computer players.

Which game to play?

GamePlayersWhat it’s like
4212 to 6Three dice and tokens to pass around: fast, a bit cheeky, with your own house rules.
The Courtier (Farkle)2 to 6Six dice and one question: roll again or bank?
Coups Fourrés2 to 4421 with a deck of cards, traps and bluffing, for strategy lovers.
Poker menteur (liar’s dice)2 to 6Five poker dice under one cup passed around: call a hand, bluff, or shout “Liar!”.

Nothing to install

Everything runs in the browser, on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac or Linux, and players at a table can mix devices. For a game without stakes, anyone can sit down just by choosing a name. A free account, with no email address, adds a friends list, messages and progress kept across devices.

Game-night ideas

  • Start a video call and keep the game in another window: the dice in the game, the laughter on the call.
  • Pick the Blitz format in 421 for a quick game (11 tokens, 10 seconds per decision).
  • Can’t all be there at once? 421 friend challenges give both players exactly the same dice: each plays within 72 hours.
  • In 421 you can also play on one phone passed around the table, or use your real dice and let the app keep score.

Nobody can rig the dice

In online games the dice are drawn by the server, not by anyone’s phone, and everyone sees the same result.

Frequently asked questions

How can I play dice with friends online?

Open a private table on 421online.com and send your friends the code or link. They join in their browser, with nothing to install.

Can we play without an account?

Yes, for a game with no stakes: everyone just picks a name.

How many players can join?

Up to 6 at a 421, Farkle or Poker menteur table, and up to 4 at Coups Fourrés.
