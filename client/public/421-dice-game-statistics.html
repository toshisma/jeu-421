How often does 4-2-1 really come up? When do people play the most? This page publishes the real statistics of 421online.com, worked out automatically from games played on the site. Totals only: no player can be identified.

Updated November 13, 2026

The figures are computed by the game server.

Where these figures come from

  • Games: only games that actually finished are counted, each one once.
  • Hands: online, every die is drawn by the server from a cryptographic source of randomness. Only rolls of all three dice at once (no die kept) are compared with theory; exact probabilities are worked out over the 216 possible rolls.
  • Countries: worked out without IP addresses, from the device time zone or language; a country only appears above a minimum number of players.
  • Dates: some counters started later than others; each one shows its start date.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these 421 statistics come from?

From games played on 421online.com, counted by the game server and recalculated automatically every hour. They are anonymous totals.

What are the odds of rolling 4-2-1 in one throw?

Six rolls out of 216, i.e. 1 in 36 (about 2.8%) for a single throw of the three dice.

Are the site’s dice rigged?

No: online, every die is drawn by the server. The real frequency of each hand, published here, gets closer to the exact probability as rolls add up.
