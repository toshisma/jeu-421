What is 421?

In France, 421 is what Liar’s Dice or Yahtzee are elsewhere: the game people play between two coffees. It’s named after its best roll, . Everyone rolls three dice, the worst hand of the round takes tokens, and you want to get rid of yours before anyone else. A game takes a few minutes, and everybody plays every round, so nobody’s stuck waiting.

How it plays

  1. Each round, roll three dice up to 3 times, keeping the dice you like. The first player of the round sets how many rolls everyone else gets.
  2. The lowest hand loses the round, and the highest hand decides how many tokens change hands.
  3. First you loading, taking tokens from a pot of 21; then you unloading, passing your tokens to the loser. Out of tokens: you’re safe. Last one holding tokens loses.

The full rules, with an example, are on the 421 rules page. The game counts the tokens for you, and a guided game teaches you the ropes.

Fair dice

Online, every die is drawn by the server with a cryptographic random source, so no player can influence a result. The 3D tray then replays that draw so everyone sees the same dice tumble.
