How it works in 30 seconds

  1. Shake the cup: only you see your five dice (9, 10, jack, queen, king, ace).
  2. Call a hand to the next player, true or not: “pair of queens”, “full house, kings over tens”…
  3. They believe you: they take the cup, look at the dice, may re-roll once (0 to 5 dice), then must call a higher hand to the next player.
  4. Or they shout “Liar!”: the cup is lifted. If the dice are at least what you called, they lose a life; otherwise, you do.

The last player with lives left wins. Hand rankings, example calls and bluffing tips are on the rules page.

Not the Perudo version

“Liar’s dice” covers a whole family of games. The best-known one, Perudo (or Dudo), gives every player their own cup and has everyone bid on all the dice on the table. This is the other branch, sometimes called “common hand” liar’s dice: one cup of five poker dice is passed from player to player, and each call is a poker hand. If you’re after the multi-cup bidding game, this isn’t it.

Ways to play

Against the computer

Opponents at 3 levels, to learn the rules, then to practise spotting bluffs (and pulling them off).

Online with friends

2 to 6 players around the same virtual cup, each on their own phone or computer. The Poker menteur menu has online games and settings.

No waiting around

Online, everyone has 30 seconds to believe, re-roll and call. After that, the game plays for them: it believes, re-rolls nothing and calls the next hand up.

Game settings

  • Lives: 3 each by default, or 5 for a longer game (private game or against the computer).
  • “No straights” variant: low and high straights don’t exist. Off by default.
  • Quick calls: the game suggests the hand just above the one you received; a simple picker (hand type, then values) lets you go higher in two taps.

Nobody sees your dice

The dice are drawn by the server, and what’s under the cup is sent only to the player holding it: not to the other players, not to the game history, until the cup is lifted. Every call is checked (it must beat the previous one), and a re-roll can only re-roll real dice, never choose their values.

Frequently asked questions

Is this liar’s dice game free?

Yes: no subscription, no ads, no purchases and no betting. It runs in the browser on phones and computers.

Is it the same as Perudo?

No. In Perudo every player has their own dice and bids on all the dice on the table. Here a single cup of five poker dice is passed around and each call is a poker hand.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes, against computer opponents at 3 difficulty levels.

How many players can play online?

From 2 to 6 players around the same cup.

How many lives does each player have?

3 by default, or 5 if you choose it before a private game or a game against the computer. The last player with lives left wins.
