The basic rules

Like the traditional game, the base rules work like this: roll six dice, set aside at least one scoring die, then roll the rest again or bank your points. A roll with nothing to score loses your points for the turn. Set aside all six dice and you can roll all six again.

The common scoring: a 1 is worth 100, a 5 is worth 50, three of a kind is the face × 100 (three 1s: 1,000), each extra matching die doubles the value, and straights are worth 500 (1 to 5), 750 (2 to 6) and 1,500 (1 to 6). See every combination on the Farkle rules page. Details can vary between versions; on 421online.com, the first player to reach the target when banking wins immediately.

What the video game adds

In the video game, the dice game is part of a role-playing world: it adds wagers in the game’s money, special dice that don’t roll like ordinary ones, and collectible items that change the game. The Courtier has none of those: it’s played with six ordinary, fair dice. You can stake play money if you want, never real money.

Play it with friends

Want a game of dice without launching the video game, or to show it to friends who don’t own it? On 421online.com, The Courtier lets you play Farkle:

  • for free, with no ads or purchases, in a phone or computer browser;
  • with friends online, 2 to 6 players at a table you share with a code or a link;
  • against the computer, 1 to 3 opponents at three levels;
  • to 2,000, 4,000, 10,000 points, depending on how much time you have.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 dice game the same as Farkle?

It’s based on the traditional game of Farkle and uses its basic rules, with extra elements of its own. It’s not exactly the same game.

Can I play the KCD2 dice game online with friends?

You can play its traditional version: on 421online.com, open a table in The Courtier and send your friends the code, for 2 to 6 players. This is not the video game itself, which is single-player.

Is this an official Kingdom Come: Deliverance game?

No. 421online.com is an independent website, not affiliated with Warhorse Studios, Deep Silver or Plaion, and this is the traditional game that inspired the video game.

Are the special dice from the video game included?

No. The Courtier uses six ordinary, fair dice, and has no items with special effects.

Is it in English?

Yes. The game is available in English, French, German and Dutch.
