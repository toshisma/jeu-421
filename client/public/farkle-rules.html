Farkle rules and scoring

Farkle is played with six dice and at least two players. The goal: be the first to reach the target score. These are the rules exactly as The Courtier applies them on 421online.com.

A turn

  1. Roll all six dice.
  2. Set aside at least one scoring die. You can set aside several, but every die you set aside must score: a 1, a 5, or a die that’s part of a combination from that roll.
  3. Choose: roll again with the remaining dice, or bank. Banking adds all the points you set aside this turn to your score and ends your turn.
  4. If you roll again, repeat step 2 with fewer dice, until you bank or farkle.

2s, 3s, 4s and 6s score nothing on their own: they only count as three of a kind or more, or in a straight.

A combination only counts if its dice come up in the same roll: a 5 set aside, then two 5s on the next roll, do not make three 5s.

Farkle

When a roll has no scoring dice, that’s a farkle: all the points you set aside this turn are lost, and play passes to the next player. Points you banked on earlier turns are safe.

Hot dice

If all six dice have been set aside during your turn, all scoring, you have hot dice: you can pick up all six and roll again, keeping the points you’ve built up. You can also bank instead. There’s no limit: a turn can chain several hot dice, or lose it all on the next roll.

End of the game

You choose the target before the game: 2,000, 4,000, 10,000 points. The first player to reach it when banking wins immediately and the game ends: there is no final round for the other players. There’s no minimum to get on the board: you can bank 50 points on your very first turn.

Common variants

House rules vary a lot. These are not used in The Courtier, so agree on them first if you play at a real table: a minimum score to get started (often 500 or 1,000), three pairs as a combination, a penalty for three farkles in a row, a final round once someone reaches the target, or different values for four of a kind and straights.

Frequently asked questions

How much is three of a kind worth in Farkle?

The face value × 100 (three 4s = 400), except three 1s, worth 1,000. Each extra matching die doubles the value.

What is a farkle?

A roll with no scoring dice. You lose every point you set aside during that turn, and play passes to the next player.

What are hot dice?

When all six dice have been set aside during a turn, all scoring. You can roll all six again and keep building your score.

Do you need a minimum score to get on the board?

Not in The Courtier: you can bank from your first turn, even 50 points. Some house rules ask for 500 or 1,000 to start.

Do three pairs score in Farkle?

Not here: only the 1s and 5s of a roll like 1-1-2-2-3-3 score. In some house rules three pairs count as a combination.

Does the other player get a last turn?

No: the first player to reach the target when banking wins immediately.
