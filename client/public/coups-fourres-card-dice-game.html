Coups Fourrés: 421 with cards

The idea

  • Each player starts with 11 tokens. The first player with none left wins.
  • Each round you roll three dice up to 3 times, with the same hands as 421.
  • The best hand wins the round, and that player gives the loser as many tokens as the hand is worth.
  • Between rolls you play cards, paying with Pints (“rounds” of drinks, the small change of each game): 1 a round, 2 for whoever just lost.
  • You can see your opponent’s Pints, hand size and trap count… but not which traps. That’s where the bluffing starts.

A round

  1. Serve: each player draws a card and gets their Pints (6 in reserve at most).
  2. The leader (last round’s loser) rolls and plays cards. Their number of rolls is the other player’s limit.
  3. The other player plays their turn, knowing the hand to beat.
  4. Last word: both hands are on the table, and the losing player can still play a “last word” card.
  5. Settling up: the winner hands tokens to the loser, cards included.

Every rule is on the Coups Fourrés rules page.

Five kinds of card

KindWhen you play it
StrikeOn your turn, before or after a roll, then discarded.
TrapPlaced face down on your turn; it goes off by itself when its condition arises.
RiposteAt any time, in answer to an opposing card.
Last wordOnly in the “last word” step, just before the tokens are settled.
RegularOn your turn; a regular stays in front of you for 3 rounds (3 at most).

Four card families

The 90 cards come in four families. Regulars bring allies and extra Pints, Drunken cards make dice spin and bump your opponent’s, Traps are placed face down, and Chains combine draws, discounts and ever stronger answers. A deck holds 2 families at most, and your opponent’s families are visible from the start.

Ways to play

Guided game

Three minutes against the computer, one step at a time.

Against the computer

Three opponents of rising difficulty, even with no account.

With friends

A coded table for 2 to 4 players (free account).

Ranked and tournament

Ranked duels in a monthly league, and a free knockout tournament every Saturday evening at 20:40 (Brussels time).

Collection and decks

Coups Fourrés is also a small collectible card game: boosters hold random cards and are opened with capsules earned by playing. They can’t be bought, and there’s nothing to pay for. Build up to 5 decks, or let the game build one from your collection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Coups Fourrés free?

Yes. Capsules are earned by playing and can’t be bought, and there are no ads or subscriptions.

Do I need to know 421?

No. The guided game explains the dice and the cards in three minutes.

How many players?

A duel against the computer, in ranked or in a tournament; 2 to 4 at a table with friends.

How long does a game take?

A duel lasts a few minutes. It can never go beyond 20 rounds.
