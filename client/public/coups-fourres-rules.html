The rules of Coups Fourrés

The goal

You play 421 in unloading only: everyone starts with 11 tokens (9 with 3 or 4 players). The first player with no tokens left wins the game.

Setup

Each player shuffles a 20-card deck and draws 5. You can send up to 2 cards back under the deck and redraw as many, once. The first leader is drawn at random.

A round

  1. Serve: draw 1 card and receive 1 Pint (2 for the loser of the previous round). You can save up to 6.
  2. Bar order: a random bonus rule applies to everyone for the round.
  3. Leader’s turn: roll up to 3 times, keep dice between rolls, and stop whenever you like. Cards can be played before or after each roll. The number of rolls sets the other player’s limit.
  4. The other player’s turn, within that limit, seeing the leader’s result.
  5. Last word: the player losing at that moment may play a “last word” card, the other may answer, and so on until both pass.
  6. Settling up: the winner gives the loser as many tokens as the hand is worth, cards included. On a perfect tie, nobody gives anything.
  7. The loser leads the next round.

The hands

The same as 421, from best to worst:

HandExampleValue
4-2-18 tokens

Two 1s + x, then three x … x tokens (6 down to 2)
Straight 2 tokens
Any other hand 1 token
Nénette (the worst)the loser takes at least 2 tokens

Pints

Pints pay for cards: the number in the small glass on each card is its cost. You receive them with each serve, and the amount each player holds is visible to all. An opponent with none can hardly play anything; one with four or more may be planning something big.

Traps, regulars and ripostes

  • Traps: 3 at most in front of any player, 3 placed at most per player. You see how many are down, never which. A trap that hasn’t gone off in 3 rounds returns to its owner’s hand.
  • Regulars: 3 in play at most, each for 3 rounds unless chased away.
  • Ripostes: when several cards answer each other, the last one played applies first.

End of the game

  • As soon as a player has no tokens left, they win.
  • If a game drags on, the bar closes after round 20: the player with the fewest tokens wins.
  • Empty deck: reshuffle your discard pile and take 1 token.
  • You have 30 seconds per decision, plus a 60-second reserve for the whole game. In ranked, leaving counts as a defeat; after a disconnection you have 60 seconds to return.

With 3 or 4 players

At a coded table with friends: 9 tokens each. The first player at 0 wins and the game stops. Only the worst hand of the round receives tokens, from the best hand, as in 421. Traps are placed in front of a chosen player, for all to see.

Building a deck

A deck holds exactly 20 cards: 2 copies at most of a card, 2 families at most, and one legendary card. You can save up to 5 decks.

Frequently asked questions

How many tokens do you start with in Coups Fourrés?

11 each in a duel, 9 each with 3 or 4 players. The first to have none left wins.

How many cards are in a deck?

Exactly 20, with 2 copies at most per card, 2 families at most and one legendary card.

Can you see your opponent’s traps?

You can see how many are down, but not which ones.

What happens on a tie?

On a perfect tie between the two hands, nobody gives any tokens.
